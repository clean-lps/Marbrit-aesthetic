# DESIGN - Marbrit Aesthetic

## Reference Board
- Structure: asymmetrical editorial landing with a clear text-first hero and real treatment photography
- Visual language: warm coral, rose metal, deep navy, soft ivory, glossy clinic photography
- Motion: short fade-and-lift entrances, no theatrical scroll tricks
- Proof: real studio images, live booking link, social channels, review CTA
- Micro-interactions: pill buttons, underlined text links, soft image hover lift
- Avoid: generic medspa cards, purple gradients, fake counters, stock spa imagery, abstract blobs

## Creative Directions
### Direction A - Coral Precision
- Why it fits: balances beauty warmth with clinical trust, which matches the brand better than a soft spa-only look
- Hero idea: real treatment image plus exact offer line for facials, laser and Venus Legacy in Coral Gables
- Section rhythm: bold hero, treatment atlas, proof band, booking section, visit/footer
- Typography direction: elegant serif display with clean sans body
- Color direction: coral metal, navy ink, tinted pearl neutrals
- Image/media direction: real close-ups of treatments, specialist portrait, clinic environment
- Motion direction: subtle reveals and image lift only
- Proof strategy: show live links, real services, current studio visuals, public social presence
- Avoid: over-romantic beauty editorial that hides the medical-aesthetic credibility

### Direction B - Atelier Glow
- Why it fits: pushes femininity and personal care harder for a more boutique beauty-studio feel
- Hero idea: specialist portrait in the studio, oversized typography, layered floral texture
- Section rhythm: softer pacing with more white space and quote-driven sections
- Typography direction: higher-contrast serif with delicate supporting copy
- Color direction: blush, oat, rose gold, smoke
- Image/media direction: studio portraiture and close-up skin texture
- Motion direction: almost invisible, mostly fades
- Proof strategy: personal attention, comfort, WhatsApp-first communication
- Avoid: becoming too soft to support laser/body-treatment authority

### Direction C - Sculpt And Signal
- Why it fits: foregrounds body contouring and device-led treatments for stronger performance energy
- Hero idea: body-treatment scene with darker, higher-contrast framing
- Section rhythm: fast, high-contrast blocks with strong CTA repetition
- Typography direction: assertive serif plus compact sans
- Color direction: deep graphite, coral signal, pale stone
- Image/media direction: devices, body contouring visuals, procedural moments
- Motion direction: crisp reveals, stronger contrast changes
- Proof strategy: treatment names, package logic, booking urgency
- Avoid: feeling too hard-edged for facial and care-led clients

## Chosen Direction
- Selected: `Direction A - Coral Precision`
- Why: it is the best midpoint between feminine beauty branding and treatment credibility, and it lets the real clinic imagery do the heavy lifting instead of decorative styling

## Design System
- Display font: `Playfair Display`
- Body font: `Geist`
- Primary background: warm pearl
- Ink color: deep navy-black
- Brand accent: coral rose
- Secondary accent: muted champagne
- Radius scale: mostly medium and large, nothing cartoon-soft
- Elevation: soft shadows with subtle navy tint, used sparingly
- Motion: 0.7s to 1.1s entrances, `ease-out`, reduced-motion fallback
- Button language:
  - Primary: filled coral
  - Secondary: dark ink
  - Tertiary: underlined text link
- Section spacing: generous vertical rhythm with alternating dense and airy blocks
- Image treatment: preserve real photography, add slight desaturation to backgrounds, keep treatment details visible
